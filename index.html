How to play

1. The goal

Night Has Come is a game of hidden roles. A small group of Mafia hides among the town. Everyone plays on their own phone, in a shared room, over several real days.

  • Citizens win as soon as every mafia member is dead.
  • Mafia win only when every non-mafia player is dead, so only mafia remain alive.

The Doctor, the Police and the plain Citizens all play for the Citizens' side.

2. Roles

Roles are dealt randomly when the game starts. You see only your own role, in your inbox. Nobody else knows it until you die.

  • Mafia: you know your teammates. Each night you choose someone to kill.
  • Doctor: once every 2 days you can save the player the town votes out.
  • Police: once every 2 days you can learn whether one player is mafia.
  • Citizen: no special power. You have your vote, your voice, and your judgment.

3. Rooms and starting

  • Someone creates a room and picks the number of players. The room gets a 6-character token to share.
  • Everyone else joins with that token and a name of up to 16 characters. You can be in several rooms at once and switch between them with the room list button.
  • Before the game starts you can leave the room. The host can also delete it at any time, which cancels the game for everyone.
  • The host (the first player) can press Start only when the room is completely full.
  • Day 1 is the calendar day the host starts the game. Days then count up at midnight.
  • All times in the game, including the times on inbox messages, follow the room's time zone (the host's time zone when the game started). Every player sees the same time, even if their phone is set to a different zone.

4. A day at a glance

8:00 AMMorning. The night's kill is announced. Voting and town chat open. Doctor and Police may use their abilities (from Day 2).
MidnightVoting closes and the vote is resolved: the executed player is announced. The alarm sounds, the town chat closes, and the mafia chat opens.
00:00 to 8:00Night. The mafia choose their victim (from Day 2). Everyone else sleeps.

If the game is started before 8:00 AM, voting simply waits until 8:00. Nobody can be killed at night before the first midnight after Day 1, so the mafia's first kill is announced on the morning of Day 2.

5. Voting (8:00 AM to midnight)

  • Every living player has exactly one vote per day. A vote is final and cannot be changed.
  • You can vote for any living player except yourself.
  • Votes are not secret. Everyone's inbox gets a message like "A voted for B" for each vote.
  • Once you have voted, you can see how many votes each player has so far.
  • At midnight, the player with the most votes is executed, and their role is revealed to everyone.
  • Tie: if two or more players finish with the same top number of votes, one of the tied players is executed at random. The inbox message says it was a random pick after a tie.
  • Nobody voted: one non-mafia player is executed at random (only from the Citizens, the Doctor and the Police, never a mafia member). The inbox message says it was a random pick because nobody voted.
  • Reminder: one hour before midnight, if you are alive and have not voted yet, a reminder arrives in your inbox (once per day).

6. The night (midnight to 8:00 AM)

At midnight the alarm sounds. Town chat and voting are closed until 8:00 AM.

  • From the first midnight after Day 1 onward, every living mafia member picks one living non-mafia player to kill. Each mafia member gets one pick per night, and it is final.
  • Mafia teammates see each other's picks, shown as "Mafia wants this one" with a vote count.
  • The target with the most mafia votes is killed. Mafia who did not vote simply do not count.
  • At 8:00 AM the victim is announced as found dead, and their role is revealed.
  • Penalty for disagreement: if the mafia tie between targets, or nobody votes, then one mafia member is executed at random instead, and their role is revealed. The Doctor cannot save them.

7. The Doctor

  • From Day 2, during the day (8:00 AM to midnight), you get a "Use ability" button. It needs no target.
  • If you use it, then whoever is about to be executed that day is saved (the player with the most votes, or the player picked at random after a tie or an empty vote), and nobody is executed at midnight. This works even if the saved player is you, and even if you voted for them.
  • It only stops the daytime execution. It cannot stop the mafia's night kill.
  • You must still be alive at midnight for the save to work.
  • Then a 48-hour cooldown: used on Day 2 means ready again on Day 4.

8. The Police

  • From Day 2, during the day, select a living player and press Investigate. You learn immediately whether they are mafia. The answer is private and stays marked on their card.
  • You cannot investigate yourself, or a player you already investigated.
  • Then a 48-hour cooldown, the same as the Doctor.

Everyone is told the next morning that the Doctor or Police used their ability, but never who the target was.

9. Chat

  • Town chat: open from 8:00 AM to midnight, for all living players. During the night it stays readable but you cannot post.
  • Mafia chat: a private channel only mafia can use. Everyone can see the tab, but only mafia can open it. It opens at midnight so they can agree on a target, and closes at 8:00 AM.
  • Messages are limited to 140 characters.
  • The Chat button shows a red number of unread messages, just like the Inbox. It clears when you open the chat. Mafia players see the total of both tabs, and each tab shows its own count.
  • Dead players cannot vote, chat, or use abilities, but they can keep following the game.

10. Winning and the end

  • The game is checked after every execution and every night death, so it can end at midnight or at 8:00 AM.
  • When it ends, everyone sees who won and who the mafia were.
  • A finished room stays open for a while so everyone can read the result. A countdown shows when it closes. It is deleted at the end of the day after the game ends.

11. Inbox and sound

  • The Inbox collects everything: your role with a short guide to it, votes, vote reminders, executions, deaths, ability announcements, investigation results, and the final result. The Deaths list on the game screen shows the latest five.
  • New messages pop up on screen, and sounds can be switched on or off in Settings (the gear on the home screen): sound effects, inbox sound, and midnight music, each separately.

12. Tips

  • Citizens: watch who defends whom, and how people vote. All votes are public. Always vote: a skipped vote helps the mafia, and if nobody votes at all, a random non-mafia player is executed.
  • Mafia: agree in your private chat before 8:00 AM. A tie costs you a teammate.
  • Doctor: use your ability on a day when a suspect you trust is likely to be targeted by the vote.
  • Police: decide when to reveal what you know. Once you do, the mafia know who you are.
